# Pilot follow-ups

Use sample data for the Finquify demo. Keep the current forms for community
details and resident onboarding while the pilot tests those workflows.

| Area | Demo data | Revisit after the demo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Name, street and number, municipality | Add a postal code when completing the address becomes useful. Consider an optional block or entrance field for buildings that share an address. Review tax ID and billing details when a billing or document workflow needs them. |
| Resident | Name, email and unit | Review the DNI or other identity document, phone number and additional contact details when a specific workflow requires them. |

Before requiring a street address and municipality in the database, verify and
complete those fields for every existing community and update the community
creation workflow. Keep the database change separate from the demo.

## Data protection before using real resident data

- Review the [GDPR checklist](proteccio-dades.md), including the processor
  agreements, hosting and transfers, and access controls for administrators.
- Prepare the privacy notice and decide where residents will see it, including
  when an administrator creates their account. Document how residents can
  exercise their rights and who handles those requests.
- For any new personal data field, document why the workflow needs it, the
  applicable lawful basis, who can access it, and how long it is kept. Define
  how to delete or anonymize the data when it is no longer needed. Apply the
  data minimization and transparency principles in
  [GDPR Articles 5, 13 and 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679).
